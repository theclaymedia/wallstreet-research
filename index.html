About WallStreet Research

A top ranked independent research firm with a history spanning four decades.

WallStreet Research (WSR), an affiliate of Alan Stone & Company, LLC, provides the global investment community with independent analyst research reports, corporate profiles and newsletters of selected quality emerging growth companies.

With the #1 ranking on Google, Yahoo and Bing search engines, WallStreet Research effectively initiates coverage on emerging companies that offer remarkable gain potential. This has resulted in WSR achieving one of the best track records in the small cap investment marketplace. Our distribution is extensive and global, far outpacing our competitors, due to our long history, superior brand name, extensive proprietary database, our #1 search engine rankings and strong capital position.

WSR is renowned for bringing undervalued companies to the attention of the investment community and enhancing shareholder value while providing liquidity to small cap companies. The boutique is spearheaded by Alan Stone, Managing Director of Alan Stone & Company, LLC, and is backed by highly regarded analysts and CFAs.

Managing Director

Alan Stone

During the past 25 years, Alan Stone & Company, LLC has become one of the top ranked investment consulting firms serving emerging companies. Alan has been involved in a wide variety of investor relations, investment banking, corporate finance and merger and acquisition activities and has arranged numerous $2 to $25 million financing and merger transactions for NASDAQ, AMEX, NYSE, OTC Markets and TSX listed companies.

Mr. Stone was Director of the Southern California AMEX Corporate Focus and Security Analyst Forum. He was an analyst and assistant portfolio manager of Merrill Lynch Asset Management's $250 million high yield corporate bond fund, later joining Merrill Lynch's brokerage subsidiary, and was also associated with Thomson McKinnon Securities and Ladenburg, Thalmann in New York City.

Alan holds a BS in Economics and Finance from the University of Pennsylvania's Wharton School and an MBA in Finance and Investments from New York University's Graduate School of Business, with advanced studies at the London School of Economics and UCLA. He is an active member of the Penn Club in New York City and has served on the board of the University of Pennsylvania Alumni Association of South Florida.
